# Annapuranam Elegance

Build a Premium South Indian Catering Website — Annapuranam Catering

Create a complete, modern, premium and visually stunning responsive website for Annapuranam Catering, a South Indian catering brand offering both Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian catering for weddings, birthdays, family functions, corporate events and special occasions.

The website should feel like a premium Indian catering brand, combining authentic South Indian tradition with modern luxury web design.

Do NOT make it look like a generic restaurant, food delivery or template website.

The overall experience should communicate:

Traditional Soul • Modern Luxury • Exceptional Food • Warm Hospitality

---

1. VISUAL IDENTITY

Use a sophisticated and warm color palette:

- Deep Forest Green — primary

- Warm Gold — premium accent

- Ivory / Cream — main background

- Dark Charcoal — text

- Subtle Terracotta — secondary accent

Keep the color usage elegant and minimal.

Typography

Use:

- Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond for elegant headings

- Poppins or Inter for body text and UI

Use large editorial-style headings, generous whitespace and strong visual hierarchy.

19. FOOD PHOTOGRAPHY — CRITICAL

Food photography is one of the most important parts of this website.

Use a consistent premium South Indian food photography style.

Images should have:

- Photorealistic appearance

- High resolution

- Warm natural lighting

- Rich realistic textures

- Appetizing presentation

- Close-up compositions

- Banana leaves

- Brass/copper vessels

- Traditional South Indian presentation

- Professional catering photography

Vegetarian images:

South Indian meals, sambar, rasam, poriyal, paneer dishes, sweets and traditional feast.

Non-vegetarian images:

Chicken biryani, mutton biryani, chicken 65, mutton curry, fish dishes and other South Indian favourites.

Desserts:

Payasam and traditional Indian sweets.

Avoid:

- Cartoon imagery

- Flat illustrations

- Generic Western food

- Low-quality stock photography

- Artificial-looking food

- Excessive filters

- Oversaturated colors

Food should look real enough to make visitors hungry.

21. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Make the entire website fully responsive.

Desktop

Use:

- Large photography

- Spacious layouts

Mobile

Use:

- Hamburger navigation

- Large readable text

- Full-width food cards

- Touch-friendly buttons

- Proper form fields

Make mobile feel intentionally designed, not just a compressed desktop version.

24. COMPONENT & TECHNICAL STRUCTURE

Build using clean reusable components.

Create reusable components for:

- Navbar

- Hero

- FoodCard

- ServiceCard

- MenuFilter

- Gallery

- TestimonialCard

- CateringPlanner

- ContactForm

- Footer

- CTA sections

Keep the code/component structure clean and maintainable.

Make navigation links functional.

Make menu filters interactive.

Make gallery images open in a lightbox.

Use placeholder images/content where necessary, but structure everything so actual business information and food photographs can easily be replaced later.

Do not invent real business claims.

Prioritize accessibility, responsiveness, performance and clean UI.

Easy enquiry is the goal.
